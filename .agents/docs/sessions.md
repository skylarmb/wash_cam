# Sessions

`wash_cam.sessions` turns per-frame bay counts from `image_detections` into vehicle visits in the `sessions` table (migration `004`). `build_sessions(bay_id, frames, interval)` walks one bay's time-ordered `(UTC time, count)` frames: a bay is occupied at count >= 1; a session opens after `OPEN_FRAMES` (2) occupied frames in a row and closes after `CLOSE_FRAMES` (2) empty frames in a row, so single-frame phantoms and misses are debounced (the cost: two cars one empty frame apart merge). A pause longer than `MAX_GAP_INTERVALS` (3) times the capture interval ends an open session with `end_reason = gap`; a session still occupied at the latest frame is `open`. `started_at`/`ended_at` are estimated halfway to the neighboring empty frame (or the frame itself after a gap), `first_frame_at`/`last_frame_at` are the raw occupied frames, and `duration_s` is `ended_at - started_at`. Occupied bay time is an upper bound on paid time.

Frames are ordered by `image_detections.captured_at` (migration `003`), UTC derived from the local-time filename by `watch.capture_time`, using the file's modification time to resolve the hour repeated when daylight saving ends. All times are stored as UTC `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS.SSS` (`sessions.format_time`) and handled as aware UTC datetimes.

The watcher backfills missing capture times at start and calls `update_sessions` after every pass with new detections: closed sessions are final, so each bay resumes after its last closed session and only open sessions are recomputed. `wash-cam sessions --rebuild` recomputes everything; use it after changing thresholds, the detector, or detections older than existing sessions. `CAPTURE_INTERVAL` (15 s) in `wash_cam/config.py` is passed to the capture loop by `scripts/install_services.sh`.

`wash-cam score-sessions [--open N] [--close N]` (`wash_cam.session_eval`) validates sessions against human labels. It pairs each labeled capture with its timestamped detection, builds true sessions from the labels with no debouncing (`build_sessions(..., open_frames=1, close_frames=1)`) and predicted sessions from the detections on exactly the same frames with the given thresholds, and matches them per bay by greatest time overlap (each prediction matches at most once). It reports per bay and overall: true, predicted, matched, missed, and phantom sessions; mean absolute start, end, and duration error in seconds; and total occupied minutes, true versus predicted, which is what revenue depends on. Label runs separated by a capture gap score as separate windows. Labels carry no reviewed flag yet, so counts carried forward in the labeling tab and saved unchecked are scored as truth.
