# Bays and evaluation

`BAYS` in `wash_cam/config.py` maps each label bay ID to a floor quad in cropped-capture coordinates (1270x340): wash bays `bay_0` to `bay_4` left to right, and the vacuum station `vac_0` in the bottom-left corner, whose quad runs to the frame's bottom edge so a car cut off by the crop still counts. Calibration keeps the crop fixed on the scene, so the quads stay valid across camera re-seats. `wash_cam.bays.assign_to_bay(box)` places a vehicle box by its bottom-center, where the wheels meet the ground, returning its bay ID, or `None` for cars queueing in the foreground or parked behind the bays. The wash bay back edges sit at y=115 rather than the painted floor line, because a car parked behind bay 3 has its box bottom near y=100 while cars in a bay have box bottoms at y=150-170. Run `uv run wash-cam bays [IMAGE ...] [--output DIR]` to redraw the quads on captures (default: every image in `tmp/images/` into git-ignored `tmp/bay_review/`) after editing them.

`uv run wash-cam evaluate --model NAME` scores a model against every verified label in `db/labels.sqlite3` whose image exists in `tmp/images/`. It reports labeled bays compared, per-bay exact accuracy, per-image exact accuracy (every labeled bay right), and mean absolute count error, overall and split into day and night. Unlabeled bays are skipped. Labels and predictions are `{bay_id: count}` mappings. Models implement the `Predictor` protocol, `predict(image) -> dict[str, int]` covering every bay, and register by name in `evaluate.MODELS`. `empty` predicts zero everywhere and is the floor any real model must beat.

`evaluate.is_night(image)` calls a capture night when more than `NIGHT_DARK_FRACTION` of its pixels fall below `DARK_PIXEL_LEVEL`. Mean brightness does not work because auto-exposure lifts night frames to daytime levels. The thresholds were tuned on a single afternoon and evening (day 22-33% dark pixels, night 41-100%) and should be rechecked as more lighting conditions are captured.
