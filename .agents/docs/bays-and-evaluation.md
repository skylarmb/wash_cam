# Bays and evaluation

`BAY_POLYGONS` in `wash_cam/config.py` holds one floor quad per wash bay, indexed left to right from 0 to match the `bay_<index>` label IDs, in cropped-capture coordinates (1270x340). Calibration keeps the crop fixed on the scene, so the quads stay valid across camera re-seats. `wash_cam.bays.assign_to_bay(box)` places a vehicle box by its bottom-center, where the wheels meet the ground, and returns `None` for cars queueing in the foreground or parked behind the bays. The back edges sit at y=115 rather than the painted floor line, because a car parked behind bay 3 has its box bottom near y=100 while cars in a bay have box bottoms at y=150-170. Run `uv run wash-cam bays [IMAGE ...] [--output DIR]` to redraw the quads on captures (default: every image in `images/` into git-ignored `tmp/bay_review/`) after editing them.

`uv run wash-cam evaluate --model NAME` scores a model against every verified label in `db/labels.sqlite3` whose image exists in `images/`. It reports labeled bays compared, per-bay exact accuracy, per-image exact accuracy (every labeled bay right), and mean absolute count error, overall and split into day and night. Unlabeled bays are skipped. Models implement the `Predictor` protocol, `predict(image) -> list[int]` with one count per bay, and register by name in `evaluate.MODELS`. `empty` predicts zero everywhere and is the floor any real model must beat.

`evaluate.is_night(image)` calls a capture night when more than `NIGHT_DARK_FRACTION` of its pixels fall below `DARK_PIXEL_LEVEL`. Mean brightness does not work because auto-exposure lifts night frames to daytime levels. The thresholds were tuned on a single afternoon and evening (day 22-33% dark pixels, night 41-100%) and should be rechecked as more lighting conditions are captured.
